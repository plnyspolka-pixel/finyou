# Architecture
- Public free-tool availability requires both an active calculator configuration and a published lead-magnet row; download/tool pages share FocusShell, while borrower outputs are ungated and investor signup/token behavior is preserved.
- Security decision (owner, 2026-10-26): public read of `seo_location_report_entries` (/raport-lokalizacje) is INTENTIONAL — it is a public SEO asset with aggregated GUS statistics only (no client data). Treat the scanner finding about its public SELECT as an accepted exception; do not restrict access or remove the page from the sitemap.
- Agent placeholder updates read the provider configuration first and merge missing defaults without deleting provider-specific keys; failed reads abort writes.
- Loan-purpose writes bind to provider phone-call metadata or a pre-existing conversation binding, never model-supplied identifiers; they return no personal data.
- All channel paths share Ania identity rules; ElevenLabs synchronization reads stored prompts only and skips missing/empty rows to avoid overwriting configured agents with defaults.
- Public Ania widgets enter reception on one browser-owned ElevenLabs socket for voice and text; provider-side transfers and mode changes keep it open, and disconnect recovery restores context without replaying uncertain turns or repeated greetings. Authenticated contextual agents remain separate.
- YouTube first comments run only after new successful queue uploads, use audience-profile configuration and a durable one-shot claim to prevent duplicates on ambiguous API outcomes; failures are isolated from publication.
- The temporary Storage manifest is isolated in its own server-only module and route; only a stored unexpired SHA-256 token hash authorizes service-role listing and signing, preventing ordinary app users from accessing export credentials.- Meta lead role is resolved per form (explicit `lead_role`, else account role/form name); investor leads use a separate capture path without loan applications so borrower automations never touch investors.
- Ania investor tools (A3 account state, A2P callback) live behind one secret-checked webhook; identity comes from the provider call metadata + SMS code or a hashed panel session token, never from model-supplied parameters.
- The temporary Storage manifest is isolated in its own server-only module and route; only a stored unexpired SHA-256 token hash authorizes service-role listing and signing, preventing ordinary app users from accessing export credentials.
- Trustpilot invitations ride on the lead magnet download email as a BCC to the AFS address stored in integration_settings; only invite.trustpilot.com addresses are accepted, first signup per material only, and a settings failure degrades to no BCC rather than a failed send.
- Reels are sales content with no risk, cost or loss sentences (owner decision 27.10.2026, replacing the one-risk-sentence rule of 26.10): the brief, series beats (no risk beat, no alert card), track rules and generator prompt forbid them at the source; the risk line lives only in the auto-applied description footer (`RISK_LINES`). The compliance reviewer never asks to add warnings and the rewriter never adds them; `autopilot/script-warnings.ts` is a post-generation backstop whose AI removal pass is accepted only when warnings drop, the CTA keyword and half the length survive and hard rules raise no new block. Hard-rule bans on promises (guarantees, "bez ryzyka", % returns) are unchanged.
- Broker vs partner (owner decision 27.10.2026): two SEPARATE account types and programs, one `affiliate_partners` row per user with `partner_kind`. BROKER (`posrednik` role, `/dla-posrednika`, `/posrednicy/rejestracja`, panel `/posrednik`): free account, no paywall/training/materials, earns ONLY a commission on a launched (disbursed) loan of a client they brought (`client_funds_disbursed`, level 1: 50% of the Finance You fee, which is a standard 7% of the loan amount = 3.5% of the loan; trigger `affiliate_on_loan_disbursed` pays broker-kind partners only); Brokers work on their OWN clients — never mention leads in broker/partner-facing copy (owner decision); no Finance You leads (leads-admin and inbox server functions are internal-staff only) and no access to the Finance You mailbox; the commission applies whether the loan is funded by an investor or by Finance You capital. CRM `/posrednik/reakcje` shows anonymised investor reactions, and `broker-feedback-tick` (pg_cron every 10 min, dedup in `broker_feedback_notifications`) emails the broker when an investor shows interest, submits a proposal, or the loan is disbursed. PARTNER (`partner` role, `/program-partnerski` linked only in the footer, panel `/partner`): earns ONLY 1000 zł (`INVESTOR_COMMISSION_PLN`) for a new investor who signs up via their link and pays the first subscription (renewals, opportunity unlocks skipped; brokers never get it). No network levels 2–3.
- Migrations live in `drizzle/migrations` (+ `meta/_journal.json` entry and a stub snapshot chained by `prevId`); `supabase/migrations` is no longer applied — do not add new files there.
- Investor webinar (owner decision 27.10.2026): recording shown every 15 min (:00/:15/:30/:45 Europe/Warsaw); late viewers join a running show only within 5 min; no forward seeking; never "na żywo"/"live" — say "pokaz". Signup reuses the lead magnet `webinar` (unpublished) with tag `webinar`; widget, form and room stay behind `WEBINAR_ENABLED=false` until the recording exists (`WEBINAR_VIDEO_ID`).
- Autopilot owner notifications (daily/weekly report, watchdog pause alarm) go by email only, never SMS (owner decision 27.10.2026): `autopilot/owner-email.ts` builds subject and text; the recipient is `OWNER_ALERT_EMAIL` or the enabled `integration_settings` row `autopilot_raporty` (`configuration.owner_email`); no address means a logged warning, not a send; max 6 owner emails per day.
- Publishing ticks must never block on the compression service or leave entries hanging: after a real "old service version" answer the service is not called for 6 h (`TRANSCODE_UNSUPPORTED_COOLDOWN_MS`, originals go out), job-less `processing` renditions older than 10 min are reclaimable, stalled FB/IG reels are resolved against Meta (`meta-stalled-reel.ts`, video id stored at upload start) instead of manual retry, publications run first in `social-publish-tick`, and Studio jobs stuck before HeyGen submission fail after 30 min.
- Bot release gate (owner decision 30.10.2026): no publish of changes touching Ania/Messenger/ElevenLabs text agents until MCP `run_ania_e2e` passes every scenario on production. It drives real `handleMessagingEvent`/`handleFeedChange` and real ElevenLabs agents with `e2e-` sender ids: Graph API sends, operator pushes and side-effect agent tools (SMS, callback scheduling, saves) are sandboxed (`src/lib/e2e-sandbox.ts`, `thread_key` prefix `e2e:`, phones +48100200…), and test leads are deleted afterwards. A turn counts only if the ElevenLabs conversation is not `failed` (mocked unit tests never caught the 1008 `thread_key` drop). Every new Messenger complaint adds a scenario in `src/lib/ania-e2e/scenarios.ts`.
- Messenger call requests („proszę zadzwonić”, „zdzwonić się”, „proszę o kontakt”) bypass the model (`call-request.server.ts`): lead → `wymaga_kontaktu`, high-urgency operator push, reply confirming the number or asking for it. Messenger follow-ups: one reminder after 24 h, 8–20 Warsaw, never after a call request (`messenger-follow-up-policy.ts`).
- Finished Studio reels (Remotion on AWS Lambda or caption-burner output, MP4 H.264 under `studio-media/mcp/studio-napisy/`) up to 60 MB skip the compression service entirely (`isStudioFinishedReelUrl` / `studioReelSkipsCompression`); compression via caption-burner on Render is only for raw library uploads, so reel publishing never depends on Render.
- Landing inwestora (owner decisions 04.11.2026): module order everywhere (menu, hero buttons, road cards, pricing) is sales network → training+mentoring (always second) → Analityk → Kancelaria → AML → Windykacja (`INVESTOR_MODULES`, no BONUS badge). The sales network is Finance You's brokerage service, never a platform: no "rezerwacja", "Karta Leada", broker/pośrednik or "bez opłat" copy, and its page has no "Jak to działa" steps; the CTA is "Złóż zlecenie poszukiwania okazji" → `ORDER_PIPELINE` = `/inwestor/umowy`. The order pipeline (abonament → dane → KYC → screening → źródło środków → umowy → Zlecenie) lives ONLY in the investor panel (`InvestorOrderPipeline` on `/inwestor/umowy`); owner decision 04.11.2026 reverted embedding it in `/sklep`/landing (no `ShopOrderPipeline`, no `returnTo: "sklep"` for Tpay/Didit returns — they always come back to `/inwestor/umowy`). `/sklep#siecsprzedazy` stays a product description with a CTA to the panel. The collection module is sold as "Windykacja" (slug `windykacja`, `/dla-inwestora/windykator-ai` 301s via `LEGACY_MODULE_SLUGS`; "Windykator AI" remains only the separate creditor product). Every module has a `quickLink` (free lesson, sample report PDF `public/marketing/przykladowy-raport-nieruchomosci.pdf` from `scripts/przykladowy-raport-nieruchomosci.ts`, free contract lead magnet, order pipeline, HIT 9,99 zł → `/inwestor/sklep#mapa-szans`, closed cases) shown under its hero button and road card. Kancelaria AI graphics carry the "E-PODPIS GRATIS" stamp (`stamp` field, `StampOverlay`). Checkmarks are `CheckIcon` (`/marketing/check-3d.webp`), not BrandIcon "check".
- Store (owner decision 04.11.2026, evening — supersedes the morning "store products" note): there is NO public storefront. `/sklep` and `/sklep/moje-zakupy` are 301 redirects to the panel store `/inwestor/sklep` (in `FREE_INVESTOR_PATHS`, so an investor without a subscription can buy; tabs `produkty` | `zakupy`, components in `src/components/inwestor/sklep/`, nav item "Sklep"). It sells ONLY: single Academy lessons (149 zł, `ACADEMY_LESSON_PRICE_PLN`, lesson 1 free), the compliance calculator HIT "Pełna mapa szans i zagrożeń — analiza finansowa pożyczki" (9,99 zł / 30 days, `CALCULATOR_CART_ID`, item_id `kalkulator:<uuid>`, 30 days from paid_at, stacking; it unlocks ONLY `/inwestor/kalkulator` — gate in subscription-gate.tsx, it does not widen `requireInvestorManagement`) and the KW report (99 zł, `KW_REPORT_PRICE_PLN`, also ordered from `/dla-inwestora/analityk-ai#raport-kw` via `KwReportOrder`). Academy module bundles, the full Academy bundle and Centrum Zarządzania are NOT sold any more (`priceItems` rejects them, no purchase CTAs); already-paid entitlements and periods stay honored (`assertEntitled`, `ownedLessonIds`, `managementModuleActive`, `requireInvestorManagement`). The cart lives in sessionStorage (`lib/storefront/cart-storage.ts`; `readStoreCart` drops unsellable items) so `/dla-inwestora/szkolenie` (`TrainingCatalog`, per-lesson buttons only) and the KW form feed `/inwestor/sklep#koszyk` through `/logowanie`. Tpay returns and the confirmation e-mail point to `/inwestor/sklep?tab=zakupy`; `tpay=success` only clears the tab's cart — access always comes from the Tpay webhook (`fulfill_store_order`). Windykator AI (`/windykator-ai`, `lib/windykator-startowy`) is a separate creditor product kept as is (`MarketingShell page="windykator"`, footer link, CI `windykator-ai-preview.yml`). Lessons map to Academy modules by the number in the title, else by sort_order (`academyModuleForCatalogLesson`) — grouping only. Lesson durations come from `training_videos.duration_seconds` (backfill: `scripts/backfill-lesson-durations.ts`).
