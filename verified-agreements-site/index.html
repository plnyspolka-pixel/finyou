Before you integrate
The important details.
Is this a qualified electronic signature?
No. This product captures electronic declarations and evidence. Qualified signatures and notarial formalities require separate providers and workflows.
Can I use it in any country?
No. Country- and document-specific preflight policies are conservative. Unreviewed document types require legal assessment.
Does the signer need an account?
No. A signer can open a private link, complete the required identity step and explicitly confirm using a one-time code.
Will agents sign instead of people?
No. Agents can orchestrate tasks via MCP; consent remains a conscious action by the signer.
